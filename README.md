A complete story in exactly six words: "She packed his suitcase. He unpacked." The first sentence sets up a departure — a relationship ending, a bag packed, a door about to close — and the second sentence reverses it in two words, leaving the reader to fill in everything in between: what was said, what changed his mind, and whether she stays. Every word earns its place, and the turn at the end is the whole story.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`.
